Features

Stock tracking

Every item gets a serial number, brand, cost, owner, and location. Edit fields directly from the table — no separate edit page needed.

Secure access

Google sign-in with a server-side whitelist — only emails your admin has registered can access the dashboard.

Stock alerts

Items below minimum or above maximum stock are flagged on the dashboard and reported automatically to a Discord channel.

Live dashboard

KPIs, recent activity, and a breakdown of inventory value by brand, updated in real time from your database.

Smart filters

Every table filters by its most useful fields — name, email, product, vendor, owner, quantity, or cost.

Dark mode

A full Dracula-themed dark mode, saved per browser and applied across every page of the app.
